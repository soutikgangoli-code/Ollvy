Three sections:

SECTION 1 - For Private Limited Company, OPC, or LLP:
- Certificate of Incorporation (mandatory)
- MOA and AOA (mandatory for Pvt Ltd and OPC)
- LLP Agreement (mandatory for LLPs, instead of MOA/AOA)
- Registered Office Address Proof - electricity/telephone/water bill or bank statement, under 2 months old (mandatory)
- Board Resolution or Authorization Letter on company letterhead (mandatory, template provided after payment)
- PAN of Authorised Signatory (mandatory)
- Aadhaar of Authorised Signatory (mandatory)

SECTION 2 - For Partnership Firm:
- Partnership Deed (mandatory)
- Certificate of Registration of Firm (optional, if registered)
- Registered Office Address Proof (mandatory)
- PAN of All Partners (mandatory)
- Aadhaar of Managing Partner (mandatory)

SECTION 3 - For Sole Proprietorship:
- Any one business registration proof - GST, Shop and Establishment, MSME, or professional tax (mandatory)
- Address proof of business premises (mandatory)
- PAN of Proprietor (mandatory)
- Aadhaar of Proprietor (mandatory)

FAQs (6):
1. Q: My company was just incorporated. Do I need PAN before bank account?
   A: Yes. Banks require company PAN. Apply immediately. Banks also accept the NSDL acknowledgement letter while physical card is in transit.

2. Q: Is company PAN different from director PAN?
   A: Yes. Company is a separate legal entity. Needs its own PAN. Each director also has their own. Different numbers for different purposes.

3. Q: How long does PAN registration take?
   A: NSDL processes in 5 to 7 working days. e-PAN arrives by email within 48 hours of allotment. Physical card takes 10 to 15 additional days.

4. Q: Can I use the acknowledgement number while waiting?
   A: Yes. Banks and the GST portal accept it. We share the acknowledgement number the same day we submit.

5. Q: My company already has PAN but I lost the card. Do I need to apply again?
   A: No. This service is for new PAN only. For lost card, apply for duplicate PAN through NSDL separately.

6. Q: Is company PAN required for GST registration?
   A: Yes. GST portal links GSTIN to company PAN. Apply for PAN first, then GST.

5 sections:
1. WHAT IS A BUSINESS PAN AND WHO NEEDS ONE - PAN is mandatory for all companies, LLPs, firms. Needed for bank account, GST, ITR. First compliance step after incorporation. Source: Section 139A Income Tax Act 1961.

2. THE DIFFERENCE BETWEEN COMPANY PAN AND DIRECTOR PAN - Company is separate legal entity, needs its own PAN. Each director has their own individual PAN. MCA does not automatically issue PAN on incorporation.

3. WHEN YOU NEED BUSINESS PAN - bank account opening, GST registration, ITR filing, TDS deduction, receiving payments where clients deduct TDS, government tenders, e-commerce platforms.

4. HOW THE APPLICATION PROCESS WORKS - Form 49A on NSDL or UTI portal. Document prep, form completion, NSDL submission, acknowledgement, verification, e-PAN delivery. 5 to 7 working days. e-PAN arrives first, physical card 10 to 15 days after. Govt fee Rs. 107 for domestic physical delivery.

5. COMMON REASONS FOR PAN APPLICATION REJECTION - name mismatch, outdated address proof, missing board resolution, invalid signatory PAN, blurry documents, incorrect CIN/LLPIN. Note: CA review before submission eliminates most rejection risk.

5 FAQs:
1. My company was incorporated yesterday. How urgently do I need PAN?
   Apply within the first week. Cannot open bank account without PAN. Use acknowledgement letter for bank while PAN processes.

2. Does an LLP need a different form?
   No. Both use Form 49A. Different supporting documents. Same timeline.

3. Can I use my personal PAN for business transactions?
   No. Use NSDL acknowledgement number where required.

4. Will company PAN be automatically linked to GST?
   Yes. GSTIN is derived from company PAN. GST portal verifies PAN during registration.

5. Does a proprietorship need a separate business PAN?
   Not always. Individual PAN can be used. But if business name differs from personal name, a business PAN avoids confusion.
